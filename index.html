About

This biblical-reference database catalogues the use of scripture in All Slave-keepers (ASK). It includes entries from the original text (O) and from subtitles editorially inserted from Lay’s table of contents (E). ASK page numbers follow the Meggitt and Rediker edition.

Help

  • Search by book, chapter or verse, for example Revelation 20:3. Use page 59 or p. 59 to search an ASK page.
  • Words can appear in different fields of the same entry. Put a phrase in double quotes to find those words together.
  • Open an entry to see its wording, references and notes. With the keyboard, tab to the entry and press Enter or Space.
  • Use author sorting to group entries by the person credited in the source. Reset clears the search and returns to ASK page order.
